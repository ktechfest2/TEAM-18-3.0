Voting extended at polling units where accreditation started late

IN INEC press releasePublished 2:48 PM · 3 min read Source
Official source. Content republished from the issuing authority and checked by the EYENEC news desk.

Polling units that began accreditation late will stay open for the full voting period. Voters already in the queue at closing time will still be allowed to vote.

Presiding officers have been told to record the actual opening time on the result sheet so it can be audited during collation.
